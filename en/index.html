Bio

The story behind the sound

Alan Aravena, known as Alan Æse, was born in Buenos Aires and raised in Santiago, Chile. From an early age he was drawn to the meeting point of rock and electronic music, and played in several projects before settling behind the decks.

Today his sound is an energetic blend of House and Tech House that, on darker stages, shifts into intense Peak Time Techno. His sets unfold like a story: immersive atmospheres, hypnotic rhythms and deep basslines that keep the dancefloor moving.

For Alan, energy comes first. Every set is packed with driving beats and, at times, vocals that capture what he wants to say at each moment of the night.
